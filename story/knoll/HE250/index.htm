Honeywell HE250A1005 humidifier

Mornings now cool enough for heating, which
provoked sneezing, watering eyes and sinus congestion.
Despite RH readings near those while dehumidifying,
  static became a thing.
Replaceable honeycomb pads are popular tech
  for central forced air HVAC humidification.

Desirable features:

  • outdoor temperature modulating RH setpoint
    to avoid window condensation.
  • easy maintenance
  • clearance lacking above furnace -> By-pass

Evaporative honeycomb pad humidification:
  • Solenoid valve opens only while HVAC blower runs.
  • Water is slowly metered into distribution tray,
    which should be installed perfectly level.
  • Water not evaporated simply drains away.
  • Heated water increases evaporation.
