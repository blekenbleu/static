static pressure Static pressure, generated by fans or blowers, moves air.
Pitot tubes measure moving air's velocity pressure, which I ignore.

Taps for static pressure, measured by manometer in inches of water,
were installed at A and B,
encompassing pressure drops for both filter and AC coil,
along with airflow direction changes (not shown),
each of which also drop pressure by 0.1 or so.

Installing tap at C will enable measuring total external static pressure.

Two 10 oz tubes of Red Devil RD-DS 181 Duct Sealant sufficed.
This took less time (< 2 hours) and was less messy than expected
(3 paper towels, 1 pair of nitrile gloves, a sacrificial 2-inch brush)

Caulking gun forced mastic beads into interior duct board seams.

Internet has little about mastic inside fiberglass duct board,
but was already used in this return:

My mastic application, by caulking gun into seams,
is mostly not visible in this image.
Mastic is both sealant and structural, after curing,
which takes up to 72 hours.
