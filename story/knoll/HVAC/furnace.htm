15KW resistance heat with 220V wiring separate from air mover;
Refrigerant lines block electric disconnects on the wall...
Heat pump coil is below furnace;
releasing its cover allows lowering furnace cover.




24VAC from transformer via fuse and condensate overflow switch
to left-most barrier strip spade connector (R)

  • R connects both heat pump and thermostat red wires
  • B connects transformer common to both heat pump and thermostat blue wires
  • G connects thermostat green wire (fan)
  • W1 connects thermostat white wire
  • W2 connects heat pump white wire
    outdoor unit signals furnace to heat for defrost mode.
  • Y1 connects both heat pump and thermostat yellow wires for compressor
  • (no connection to Y2, because no second-stage cooling)
  • O connects both heat pump and thermostat orange wires for reversing valve
