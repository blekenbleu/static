Honeywell TH6220D1002
  • lacks fan circulate mode
  • assumes single-stage heat
  • wired to Y1 instead of Y2
  • sets Aux Heat for > 1° setting increase

Assumptions

  • ASPF426016EA furnace provides 24VAC for controls,
    to which it only responds.
    full (40A) heat by W2 and half by W1.
  • GSZ130421AE heat pump sets W1 for defrost
  • OT18-60A sets W2 for temps too low for GSZ130421AE
  • Service Instructions

Sensi ST55

  • 3-stage heat (Y2, W1, W2) and 1-stage cool (Y2 + O)
  • should NOT set Aux (W1) heat for setting increases > 1
  • sets W[12] only for too slow Y2 temperature increase rates
  • If/when OT18-60A sets W2,
    ST55 drops Y2 (and W[12]) when indoor temp > setting.
