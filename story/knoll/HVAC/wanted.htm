wanted thermostat operation

Honeywell TH6220D1002
  • lacks fan circulate mode
  • assumes single-stage heat
  • wired to Y1 instead of Y2
  • switches to Aux Heat for more than 1 degree setting increase

Assumptions

  • ASPF426016EA furnace provides 24VAC for controls,
    to which it only responds.
  • ST55 should use Y2 for single-stage cool,
    so that ASPF426016EA "knows" to set full blower speed.
  • ASPF426016EA responds to W2 by full (40A) heat and to W1 by half.
  • GSZ130421AE heat pump wants W2 for defrost
  • OT18-60A wants W2 for temperatures too low for GSZ130421AE
  • Service Instructions

Sensi ST55

  • will be configured for 2-stage heat (Y2, W1) and 1-stage cool (Y2 + O)
  • should be "smart" enough to NOT set Aux (W1) heat for setting increases > 1
  • should set W1 only for too slow Y2 temperature increase rates.

  • Lacking outside temperature input, ST55 cannot know when W2 is wanted.
  • If/when OT18-60A or GSZ130421AE defrost sets W2,
    ST55 should detect indoor temp > setting and drop Y2 (and W1).
