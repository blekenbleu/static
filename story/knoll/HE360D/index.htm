Honeywell HE360D humidifier

Soon after mornings got cool enough for heat,
sneezing, watering eyes and sinus congestion increased.
Despite RH readings near those noted while dehumidifying,
static became a thing. Since moving from Kentucky,
replaceable honeycomb pads became the prevailing tech
for central forced air HVAC humidification.

Desireable features

  • outdoor temperature sensing modulates RH setpoint
    to avoid window condensation.
  • easy maintenance
  • fan-driven (instead of bypass) for heat pumps

Evaporative honeycomb pad humidification works by
  • slowly metering water over honeycomb pad,
    which must be perfectly level for best flow and evaporation
  • Water not evaporated simply drains away.
  • Supplying heated water increases evaporation.
  • Solenoid valve allows flow only while HVAC blower runs.

AprilAire is the dominant humidifier brand

  • Fan-driven 700A model has outdoor temperature sensor;
  • 720A added water recycling to reduce waste.
  • 700/720 parts differ from those for other models.
