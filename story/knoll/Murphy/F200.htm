F-200 Murphy bed frame

By now, may folks will have encountered products with both hardware and software,
but separately documented as if either were functional without the other.
Murphy Bed Depot attempts something comparable for the F-200 bed frame,
with one page (51) of not particularly useful notes that each customer
will need to sort when or whether to apply.

Meanwhile, separate F-200 assembly instructions mention the cabinet twice,
on pages 2 and 6.   While seemingly of better quality than the cabinet,

frame instructions are correspondingly worse.


"Murphy Slat Beds are packed in 2 boxes."
Which of 4 actual boxes did not count?

Section 2, step 2 - "Place the bolt heads on outside."
outside of what?
Unclear which bolt holes allow later adjustment.

Section 2, step 4

Section 4 Photo 1 - "using vice grips"
Insane;  instead use long shaft #1 Phillips screwdriver or equivalent
to pry upper spring end, first using Yoke Connector bar as a fulcrum.

Section 4 step 16
short video is wanted for "slipping" slat ends into holders.

Section 4 step 18
With springs (4 each side) to balance with mattress,
frame does not stay down without mattress,
which must be removed to add springs.
