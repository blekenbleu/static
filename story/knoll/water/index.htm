While Weaverville house plumbing is Polybutylene plastic,
plumbing around sediment filters is (better) PEX.
UV filter can be plumbed into PEX using push (SharkBite) connections,
but unions are wanted to facilitate occasional cleaning.
  • PEX cutter, depth gauge and deburring tool
    $95 plumbing fittings
  • 3/4" push fit PEX-1" male NPT to 1" male-female NPT ball valve (for filter service) to 1" union
  • union to UV filter nipple to outlet
  • UV filter inlet tp UV filter nipple to union to 1" to 3/4" bushing to 3/4" NPT street elbow
  • elbow to 3/4 x 8" pipe to second street elbow
  • 3/4" close nipple to Whirlpool WHKF-DWHV with 1 micron (or 5 micron) filter
      to 3/4 male NPT-Push-to-Connect (SharkBite Max) to PEX
